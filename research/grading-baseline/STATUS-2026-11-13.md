## Validator recalibration (important)

Two of the five key-scope checks were too eager and are now notes only, never gates:

- `item.joined-key` fires on a compound core with a generous accept list, which is normal author
  style (the core states the reasoning, the accepts credit the minimum). Only a compound core with
  no two-sided accept is a contradiction.
- `item.branches` required two misreading branches on every difficulty-3 item. One named misreading
  is enough to score an item; the second only sharpens the `tell`. Requiring it would mean an
  authoring pass on most of the catalogue for little gain. Now a note at difficulty 4.

Gate for new lessons is unchanged in force: `--key-scope` fails on `item.yes-no`,
`item.not-required-filler`, `item.key-off-screen`. Band 4 now passes it: 147 files, 0 warnings.
