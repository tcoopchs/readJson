## What the adaptation has to do

Four of the six sources sit at FK 8.7 to 12.5, well above the band 5 target of roughly 5.5 to 7.
The load is almost entirely sentence length: the *content* is already simple in all four, so the
work is splitting long sentences and cutting administrative asides, not simplifying ideas.

Every excerpt is recorded with its exact URL and access date, `text_status` says "Adapted, reading
load reduced by splitting long sentences", and `adaptation` names what was cut. No fact is added,
and no passage is written from scratch — 2.6 requires human-written basis at band 5 and up.
