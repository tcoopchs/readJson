## Source standards for later sections

- Prefer primary or government sources over summaries.
- One summary may stand in for a book only if the primary is unavailable.
- Every evidence-table row needs a page or section reference, not just a URL.
- Peer-reviewed studies get sample and population noted, because adolescent and adult findings do
  not automatically transfer.
