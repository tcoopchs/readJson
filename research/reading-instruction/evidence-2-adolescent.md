## What does not transfer from early grades

| Early-grade practice | Why it does not transfer cleanly | App consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phonics-first sequencing | Most older readers have passed basic decoding, though some still need multisyllabic work. | Do not build lessons around letter-sound drills; support difficult words in context. |
| Picture books and simple narrative | Older readers need age-appropriate content and disciplinary text. | No children's stories; adult subjects, including nonfiction processes. |
| Teacher-led oral discussion | The app has no teacher or peer group. | Convert discussion moves into stored prompts and accepted-idea sets. |
| Leveled text as the default | Easier text showed no benefit for most ninth graders in the Lupo study. | Keep the band's idea load; scaffold rather than simplify. |
| Fluency as the main outcome | Fluency is necessary but not sufficient for comprehension. | Measure comprehension moves, not reading speed. |

## Design consequences for this app

1. The passage carries the band's idea load. Scaffolds live in `help`, `tell`, and `show_part`,
   not in a simplified rewrite. [9, 13]
2. Each lesson should include at least one gist/main-idea move and one evidence-joining inference,
   because those are the comprehension-building practices with strong evidence. [9, 17]
3. Vocabulary support is inline and contextual. A hard word is either explained in the passage or
   supported by a cloze/context item; it is not left to outside knowledge. [16, 23]
4. One lesson practices one move. The session is practice, not a placement test. [22, 26]
5. Do not describe the reader as weak or the text as easy. Respect is an instructional variable
   for older readers. [24]

## Open issues from this section

- The Lupo study is one randomized trial in ninth grade; its finding should shape policy but not
  become an absolute rule.
- E13 and E23 conflict in emphasis. The resolution is that comprehension is the usual bottleneck,
  but word-level support stays available.
- No source yet on how much text difficulty is appropriate at grades 11-12 for adult readers.
